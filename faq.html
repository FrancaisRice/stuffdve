The bites

A soft pretzel bite made from dough that includes rescued, milled pretzels, hand-filled with something delicious and baked twice.

Sealed bags keep for 10 weeks in the pantry. Once open, eat within three days.

They’re great at room temperature. Eight minutes at 350°F, or four in an air fryer, brings the fillings back to molten.

All bites contain wheat and milk. Hazel Darling contains hazelnuts. Everything Schmear contains sesame. Our kitchen handles tree nuts, so we can’t guarantee any bite is nut-free.

Upcycling

Yes. We only collect pretzels that were baked the day before, stored properly and never served. They’re dried and milled before going back into dough, then baked again at high heat.

About 40% of the crust by weight comes from rescued pretzels. That’s roughly 40 grams per bag.
