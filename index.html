Every evening, good bakeries throw out perfectly good pretzels. We collect them at dawn, re-knead them into new dough, and stuff every single bite with something worth talking about.

Stuff’d. is a snack brand built on a second chance. Upcycled pretzel dough on the outside. Molten, sweet or spicy on the inside. All of it made in small batches.
