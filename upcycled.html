Nothing good gets wasted.

Every Stuff’d. bite starts life as a pretzel that a bakery couldn’t sell. We give it a second, better one.

Bread is one of the most thrown-away foods there is. Pretzels go stale fast, so at closing time good ones end up in the bin. We think that’s the wrong ending.

Crumbs.

Unsold pretzels, about to be thrown out at closing time.

Four steps, no shortcuts.

i

Collect at dawn

Our vans visit partner bakeries before sunrise and pick up yesterday’s unsold pretzels.

ii

Mill & re-knead

The pretzels are dried, milled into crumb and folded into fresh dough. Forty percent of every crust is rescued.

iii

Stuff by hand

Each bite is filled and sealed by hand, then brushed with brown butter and finished with flaked salt.

iv

Bake twice

A second bake sets the glassy, salted shell and keeps the inside soft for weeks.

One pretzel, followed.

6:10 pm

The bakery closes.

Forty-odd pretzels didn’t sell. Tomorrow they’d be too firm for the counter.

5:40 am

We knock on the back door.

Crates are swapped, weighed and logged. The bakery gets paid for every kilo.

9:00 am

Milled into new dough.

The pretzels become crumb, then become crust again. Same flavor, new life.

2 days later

In your snack drawer.

Stuffed, baked twice, bagged and on its way to you. No crumb left behind.
